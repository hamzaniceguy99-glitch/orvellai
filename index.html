How we work

No studio, no kiln, no shortcuts on safety

Orvellai is coaching only. We provide no studio, no wheel, no kiln and no firing, and we sell no clay or glazes. Most clients work at home or at a community studio, and we plan around whichever you have.

Two things we won't soften. Clay dust contains crystalline silica and is a genuine long-term respiratory hazard, so wet cleaning is not optional. And food-safe means a commercially certified, lead-free glaze fired as the manufacturer specifies — never a homemade mixture on something someone will eat from.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I learn pottery without a wheel?

Yes, and hand-building is a serious discipline rather than a consolation prize. Two of our four programs need nothing but clay and a table.

Where do I get things fired?

Community studios, colleges and some pottery shops fire work for a fee. We'll help you find options and prepare pieces the way a firing service expects.

Why does my pot always go off-center?

Almost always bracing and pressure, not strength. Film your hands from the side and it usually becomes obvious within one review.

Is clay dust really dangerous?

Yes, over years of exposure. Wet clean, don't sweep dry, and don't sand bone-dry clay indoors without proper protection. This is the part of the hobby people skip and shouldn't.

Can I make mugs to sell?

You can make functional ware with certified food-safe glazes correctly fired. Selling brings other obligations, and we'll be clear about where coaching stops.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Struggling to center?

Twenty minutes on your hands, your clay and the first adjustment. Free.
